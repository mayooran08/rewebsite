About Gevitha Kunaratnam

From dental hygienist to real estate broker: a human approach with a strategic vision.

About me

I have always been driven by the desire to help people, to build genuine human relationships and to contribute positively to their quality of life. That passion first led me to the health field, where I worked for 10 years as a dental hygienist.

That experience helped me develop qualities that still define me today: listening, empathy, rigour, attention to detail and, above all, the importance of building lasting relationships of trust. Over the years, I had the privilege of building a loyal clientele who came back specially for the quality of my services and my personalized approach.

Today, as a residential and commercial real estate broker at RE/MAX 2000, I continue the same mission in a different way: supporting people in their life projects and helping them make important decisions for their future and their assets.

I combine my human approach with a strategic vision of real estate, placing great importance on market analysis, negotiation, marketing and the protection of my clients’ interests. I believe a successful transaction rests as much on a good strategy as on a relationship of trust and transparent communication.

My philosophy is simple: every real estate project deserves the same attention, the same rigour and the same commitment as if it were my own or a family member’s. My advice and recommendations are always guided by sincerity, honesty and the desire to help you make the best possible choices.

Passionate about innovation, I also use new technologies and artificial intelligence tools in my work to explore creative strategies, showcase properties and offer a modern, personalized service.

To me, real estate goes far beyond buying or selling a property. It is above all a human relationship, a professional responsibility and a sincere commitment to the success of your projects.
