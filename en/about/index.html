About Gevitha Kunaratnam

A Laval real estate broker who listens first and advises honestly.

Buying or selling a property is one of the biggest decisions you will make. I am here to make it clear, calm and successful, from the first conversation to the day you get your keys.

As a residential and commercial broker with RE/MAX 2000 in Laval (Chomedey), I help families, first-time buyers, investors and business owners find the right property and the right deal.

I speak English, French and Tamil, so you can make decisions comfortably in the language you prefer.

What you can expect

  • Honest advice, even when it is not what you hoped to hear
  • Clear, quick communication at every step
  • Strong negotiation on your behalf
  • Service in English, French or Tamil
