Buying or selling a property is a big step, and it is normal to want some protection along the way. As a RE/MAX broker, I can offer you programs designed for exactly that. This article is a quick overview. The **official pages** carry the full terms, so please read them before you decide.

![Gevitha Kunaratnam, residential and commercial real estate broker](/assets/photos/blue-chair-900.jpg)

## Tranquilli-T: protection for your transaction

Tranquilli-T is offered at no extra charge by participating RE/MAX brokers, for eligible residential transactions made through them. It covers five areas:

- Delay and withdrawal
- Death
- Legal assistance
- Small claims
- Legal assistance to reach an amicable settlement

Eligibility conditions apply, for example the type and the price of the property.

[Read the official Tranquilli-T page](https://www.remax-quebec.com/en/tranquilli-t)

## Intégri-T: a guarantee against hidden defects

Intégri-T is offered by RE/MAX Québec in partnership with GBQ (Garantie des bâtiments du Québec). It protects buyers against latent, or hidden, defects such as problems with the roof, the foundation, the electrical or plumbing systems, or water seepage and mould. The protection lasts three years after the deed of purchase.

In general, the guarantee has to be taken **before** you sign at the notary's office, and some properties are not eligible.

[Read the official Intégri-T page](https://www.remax-quebec.com/en/integri-t)
